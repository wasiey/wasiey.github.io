1Summary

Most of my week is OneStream itself: business rules in VB.NET, workspaces and dashboards, data loads from SAP and Databricks, and the month-end close. As senior developer I also set the coding standards and review what our own developers and outside partners build before it goes to production.

The rest goes into LLM agents that sit on the OneStream REST API. One helps the admin team track down problems. The other lets finance users ask where a number comes from. Neither can change data on its own.1

Before this I spent three years on data pipelines and dashboards at a logistics company. My master's thesis used machine learning to optimise industrial systems.
