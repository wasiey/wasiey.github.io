About

I'm the technical owner of the OneStream platform at a global manufacturer, covering consolidation, IFRS group reporting and forecasting. I set the coding standards, review code from internal and external developers, and keep releases moving safely from development to production.

Lately most of my time goes into AI agents for finance: tools that trace a number back to its source, explain how it was calculated, and answer plain-language questions on live data. They only read. Any change still needs a person to approve it.

Before OneStream I worked in data engineering and analytics in logistics. My master's thesis was on machine learning for optimizing industrial systems.
